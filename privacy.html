Privacy Policy

Quran Video Uploader · last updated 18 November 2026

Quran Video Uploader is a personal tool used only by the owner of one YouTube channel to upload videos to that channel. This page explains what it does with Google account data.

What it accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the tool asks for one permission: to upload and manage videos on their YouTube channel (youtube.upload). It does not ask for, read or store email, contacts, files, viewing history or any other account data.

How that access is used

Only to upload the videos the tool makes, with their titles, descriptions, tags and publishing times, to the owner's own channel.

Where data is kept

The sign-in token is saved only on the owner's own computer. It is never sent anywhere except to Google, when uploading. The tool has no server, no database of users, no analytics, no advertising and no cookies.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with or transferred to anyone. The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the tool's secrets folder removes the saved token from the computer.

YouTube

Videos are uploaded through the YouTube API Services, so YouTube's own Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy also apply.
