Quran Video Uploader

A personal tool that publishes Quran recitation videos to one YouTube channel.

What it does

The tool runs on its owner's own computer. It turns verses of the Quran into short videos, with recitation, the Arabic text and a translation, and uploads them to the owner's YouTube channel through the official YouTube Data API.

Who uses it

Only the channel owner. It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts. Signing in with Google lets it upload videos to that one channel and nothing else.
